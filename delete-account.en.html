Delete your Akiiwork account

Developer: Jaehwan Kim · App: Akiiwork

In the app

  1. Open Akiiwork and sign in.
  2. Go to Me in the bottom tab bar.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

For accounts signed in with Apple, the Sign in with Apple connection is also revoked after Apple confirms.

Without the app

If you've removed the app or can't sign in, email us with the account you signed up with (Apple, Google or Kakao). We'll delete it without delay and let you know.

ahhancom@gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Your account identifier, sign-in details and profile (nickname, email)
  • All usage records: clock-in/out, breaks, tasks, notes and work schedule

Deleted data can't be recovered. We don't keep any of it afterwards.
