Akiiwork Privacy Policy

Akiiwork (the "Service") is operated by Jaehwan Kim (the "Operator"). This policy explains what personal information the Service collects, why, and the choices you have. The Korean version of this policy also applies to users in Korea under the Personal Information Protection Act.

Effective: November 10, 2026

  • Error reports and usage analytics are sent in a form that doesn't identify you and aren't linked to your account.

2. Why we use it (and legal bases)

  • To identify you and keep you signed in (performance of a contract)
  • To record your work and task time, show statistics, and restore your records on a new device (performance of a contract)
  • To restrict misuse (legitimate interests)
  • To find and fix errors and improve features using anonymous statistics (legitimate interests)
  • To show ads that keep the Service free; personalized ads only if you allow app tracking (consent)

3. Information visible to other users

None. The Service has no feature for viewing other users' records.

4. Retention and deletion

  • We delete your personal information without delay when you delete your account. Your profile and all records are deleted together.
  • If you disconnect Akiiwork in your Kakao account settings, your account is deleted the same way.
  • Signing out deletes the records stored on your device (records on the server stay and come back when you sign in again).
  • If the law requires us to keep some information, we keep it separately only for that period.

5. Sharing

We don't sell or share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law.

The ad SDK in the app (Google AdMob) collects the "Advertising" items in section 1 directly. Your work and task records, nickname and sign-in details are never passed to the ad SDK.

7. Personalized ads

  • On iOS, before showing ads for the first time, we ask whether you allow app tracking. Only if you allow it do we use the advertising identifier for personalized ads; otherwise you see non-personalized ads only. Every feature works the same either way.
  • To turn off personalized ads on iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking › turn off Akiiwork
  • On Android: Settings › Google › Ads › Delete advertising ID
  • We request only ads suitable for all ages.

8. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • View and edit your profile in the app (Me › Edit)
  • Delete your account (Me › Delete account)
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete, port or restrict the processing of your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Email us at the address below and we'll respond without delay. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 14 (or the minimum age required in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). We delete accounts found to belong to them.

10. Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
  • Row-level access control in the database: you can read and write only your own records
  • Least-privilege access and protected admin accounts
