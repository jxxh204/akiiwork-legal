FAQ

Can other people see my records?
No. Your records and nickname are stored only in your account, and there's no way to view other users' records.

What happens if I change phones?
Sign in with the same Apple or Google account and your records come back.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Me › Delete account. Your profile and all records are deleted right away. Account deletion
